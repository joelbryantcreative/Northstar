Who's Running This

A filmmaker who built the business backwards.

I started making films at fourteen. Built my identity in public, one project at a time, until I had documentaries selected at international film festivals and a body of work I could actually point to.

I grew my Instagram past 49,000 followers through a 30-day filmmaking challenge. Spent years working at one of the biggest YouTube channels in the world before I left to build my own story.

Somewhere along the way, I stopped separating the art from the business. That's when both of them started compounding.

I'm not interested in turning filmmakers into content marketers. I'm interested in showing you how to build a business that pays for the work you actually want to make.

Money is the tool. Art is the point.
