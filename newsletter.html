How This Started

I’m a filmmaker. I live by making things — and I’d usually rather show than tell.

But a couple of years ago I started making a habit of journaling my own thoughts every single day. It quietly became one of the most life-changing things I’ve ever done — clarity I didn’t know I was missing, ideas I wouldn’t have found any other way.

This newsletter is me sharing that with you. The lessons from each week of building my own creative business, day in and day out. Not polished. Not curated. Just real.
