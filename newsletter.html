How This Started

I'm a filmmaker. I live by making things and I'd usually rather show than tell.

But a couple of years ago I started journaling every single day. It quietly became one of the most life-changing things I've ever done. Clarity I didn't know I was missing, ideas I wouldn't have found any other way.

This newsletter is me sharing that with you. The lessons from each week of building my own creative business, day in and day out.
