Every Sunday I write from Byron Bay — the lessons nobody shares about building a creative life, ideas I can’t stop sitting with, and the real stories behind the work. Think of it as insider access to everything I’m learning, making, and figuring out along the way.

I write every word myself, and I read every reply that comes back.

Free. Every Sunday. Unsubscribe whenever.

What This Is

I’m a documentary filmmaker. I’d usually rather show than tell — but a couple of years ago I started writing every Sunday, and it’s quietly become the most honest thing I make.

Some weeks it’s a story from a shoot. Some weeks a lesson from building this. Some weeks just where my head’s at. Not always tidy. Always real — and yes, I reply to the people who write back.
